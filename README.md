This dashboard provides a complete analysis of advertising campaign performance across Facebook and Instagram, helping track campaign reach, engagement, conversions, and budget utilization.

🔑 KPIs Tracked
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Shares
- Comments
- Purchases
- Engagements
- CTR (Click Through Rate)
- Engagement Rate
- Conversion Rate
- Purchase Rate
- Total Budget Allocation

📈 Key Insights
✅ Instagram campaigns generated higher engagement and impressions compared to Facebook campaigns.
✅ Female audiences contributed the highest share of impressions and engagement.
✅ Users aged between 20–30 years were the most active and responsive audience segment.
✅ Carousel and Stories ad formats delivered stronger CTR and engagement rates.
✅ The United States and United Kingdom contributed the highest campaign reach.
✅ Peak user activity was observed during afternoon and evening hours.
✅ Weekly campaign trends remained stable with noticeable spikes during high-performing periods.

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX Measures
- Data Modeling
- Interactive Dashboard Design
- KPI Analysis
- Marketing Analytics
